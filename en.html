A LITTLE STORY

Everyday moments,
served steaming hot.

Growing up, the best mornings began with tea and dim sum around the family table. The rising steam, teacups gently clinking, and someone saying, “Let’s order one more basket.”

That warm, unhurried feeling is what we hope to share at Longli. We make each basket fresh, with good ingredients and a little extra care. Come with family, meet an old friend, or simply take a quiet moment for yourself.
